Professional Journey

With 22 years of restaurant industry experience, I've developed a passion for exceptional service and craft cocktails. Currently, I serve as a bartender at Bedford Post Tavern, a prestigious Relais & Chateaux destination known for its exceptional dining experience and sophisticated atmosphere.

My approach to bartending draws from the precision and energy of NYC-style service — fast-paced, detail-oriented, and focused on delivering memorable experiences for every guest.

What makes my background unique is the combination of extensive hospitality experience, a foundation in education as a former math teacher, and technical skills as a web developer. This intersection of expertise has allowed me to create innovative training tools for the bar industry.
