About BarToolsPro

Professional bartending solutions designed by industry experts for restaurant groups and hospitality professionals

Our Mission

BarToolsPro was founded to bridge the gap between traditional bartender training and modern technology. We understand the challenges restaurant groups face: high turnover, inconsistent training, and the need for efficient, scalable solutions.

Our platform combines real-world bartending expertise with innovative educational technology to create training tools that actually work in busy restaurant environments. Every solution is built with the understanding that comes from years behind the bar.

We're not just software developers—we're hospitality professionals who understand what it takes to run successful bar programs at scale.

Founded by Industry Professionals

Built by Bartenders, for Bartenders

BarToolsPro was founded by Adam Liscia, a professional bartender with over 22 years of restaurant industry experience. Currently serving at Bedford Post Tavern, a prestigious Relais & Chateaux destination, Adam has witnessed firsthand the training challenges that restaurant groups face.

His unique background combines:

  • Extensive hospitality experience at high-end establishments
  • Educational expertise as a former math teacher
  • Technical skills as a web developer
  • Deep understanding of NYC-style service standards
