Build a Cocktail Game

How many cocktails can you build before time runs out? Test your knowledge of cocktail recipes with this interactive training tool for bartenders.

Game Rules:

  • Time: You start with 60 seconds to complete as many cocktails as possible.
  • Tips: You earn $1 for each completed cocktail. Complete a cocktail in under 10 seconds to earn an extra $1 speed tip!
  • Mistakes: Each wrong answer costs you $1 in tips. Choose measurements carefully!
  • Time Bonus: Completing a cocktail adds time to your clock:
    • Base bonus: 3 seconds + 0.5 seconds per ingredient
    • Speed bonus: Up to 5 additional seconds for fast completion
  • Challenge: Choose from menu cocktails, non-alcoholic options, starred favorites, cocktail families, or create your own custom set.
